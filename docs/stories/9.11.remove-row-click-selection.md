# Story 9.11: Remove Row Click Selection - Checkbox Only

**Epic:** [Epic 9: Dashboard Data Display and Inline Editing](../epics/epic-9-dashboard-data-display-and-inline-editing.md)

**Status:** Review

## Story

**As a** HR Admin user,  
**I want** employee selection to work only via checkboxes (not by clicking anywhere on the row),  
**so that** I can interact with employee rows (e.g., inline editing, buttons) without accidentally selecting/deselecting employees.

## Background

In Story 13.3, row-click selection was implemented where clicking anywhere on an employee row would select/deselect that employee. This functionality is now being removed because:

- It interferes with other row interactions (inline editing, buttons, links)
- It causes accidental selections when users intend to interact with row content
- Checkbox-based selection provides clearer, more intentional selection behavior
- The checkbox is already visible and accessible, making row-click redundant

**Related Stories:**

- Story 13.2: Added checkbox-based selection (kept)
- Story 13.3: Added row-click selection (to be removed)
- Story 13.4: Export only selected employees (depends on selection working correctly)

## Acceptance Criteria

**Given** I am viewing an employee row on desktop  
**When** I click anywhere on the row (not on the checkbox, not on buttons/inputs/links)  
**Then** the row selection does NOT change  
**And** the row does not receive or lose the greyish tint  
**And** the checkbox state does not change  
**And** other row interactions (inline editing, buttons) work normally

**Given** I am viewing an employee row on desktop  
**When** I click the checkbox on the left of the row  
**Then** the employee becomes selected (if unchecked) or unselected (if checked)  
**And** the row receives or loses the greyish tint accordingly  
**And** the checkbox state updates correctly

**Given** I am viewing an employee card on mobile  
**When** I tap anywhere on the card (not on the checkbox, not on buttons/inputs/links)  
**Then** the card selection does NOT change  
**And** the card does not receive or lose the greyish tint  
**And** the checkbox state does not change  
**And** other card interactions work normally

**Given** I am viewing an employee card on mobile  
**When** I tap the checkbox on the card  
**Then** the employee becomes selected (if unchecked) or unselected (if checked)  
**And** the card receives or loses the greyish tint accordingly  
**And** the checkbox state updates correctly

**Given** I have multiple employees selected via checkboxes  
**When** I click the export button  
**Then** only the checkbox-selected employees are exported  
**And** the selection state works correctly for export operations

**Given** I am interacting with inline editable fields in an employee row  
**When** I click on the field to edit it  
**Then** the row selection does NOT change  
**And** the inline editing works normally

**Given** I am clicking buttons within an employee row (e.g., Edit, Archive, action menu)  
**When** I click the button  
**Then** the row selection does NOT change  
**And** the button's normal action executes correctly
